About COLORON

A family of craftsmen, led by Rajesh.

COLORON Painting Contractor and Decorators is a Bengaluru-based painting firm built on patience, surface preparation and finishes that still look right years later. Under the guidance of owner Rajesh, our crews have completed more than a thousand residential and commercial projects — from villa interiors to 19-floor apartment towers.

We work with leading developers and private clients across Sarjapur Road, Electronic City, Hosur Road, Hennur, Whitefield, Bannerghatta and beyond. Every site is run with the same discipline: clean masking, honest materials, and a team that treats occupied homes with care.
